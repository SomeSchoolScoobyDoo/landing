# Organisation Division — Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Organisation onboarding creates a distinct tenant with explicit owner.
- [ ] Tenant lifecycle and suspension behaviour are implemented.
- [ ] Roles and permissions are documented and enforced server-side.
- [ ] Invitations are tenant-scoped, expire, can be revoked and cannot be replayed.
- [ ] Administrators manage only their own tenant.
- [ ] Course assignment is scoped to organisation and permitted groups.
- [ ] Instructors see only assigned learners/classes and allowed fields.
- [ ] Learners cannot view organisation administration or peer private records.
- [ ] Reports enforce row-level and field-level access.
- [ ] Exports require permission, field minimisation and audit logging.
- [ ] Bulk actions require confirmation and safe limits.
- [ ] Billing contacts cannot automatically access grades/submissions.
- [ ] Member removal and role downgrade revoke permissions promptly.
- [ ] Offboarding, export, retention and deletion states are clear.
- [ ] Responsive/accessibility/error recovery verified.
- [ ] Tests cover at least two organisations, multiple roles and denied access.
