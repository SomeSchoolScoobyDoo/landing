Questions

Before you start

What courses can I take right now?

The published catalogue currently includes Computer and Internet Readiness and AI Toolkit Setup. Both are beginner-level courses.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The current courses are designed for beginners and focus on computer readiness, internet use and getting started with AI assistants.

Can I preview a course before enrolling?

Yes. Open a course from the catalogue to read its description and preview the published module and lesson titles before creating an account or enrolling.

Is learning self-paced?

Yes. You can work through the available lessons at your own pace and return to your learner dashboard to continue.

What if the learning platform is temporarily unavailable?

The academy website remains available separately. If the learning platform is temporarily unavailable, return here and try Learn again later.
