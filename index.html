Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Is learning free for individuals?

Yes. Individuals can access foundational learning for free. Professional standalone courses are separate paid programmes.

Do free courses include certificates?

Eligible free courses award a completion certificate when the learner meets the course completion requirements.

How much are professional course certificates?

The listed professional courses currently show a ₦6,000 certificate fee. Full course access and payment details will be displayed before enrolment.

Can a school or company train a group?

Yes. Schools and organizations can contact Kaizora to discuss group learning, reporting, custom programmes and institutional access.

Can I unsubscribe from emails or delete my account?

Account and email preference controls are part of the platform requirements. Email subscribers should be able to unsubscribe, and users should be able to request account deletion.
