The 4Ds of AI Fluency

Anthropic's 4Ds framework shows that prompting is only one part of being good at AI. Use the four practices to make better decisions before, during and after working with an AI system.

01

Delegation

Know what to give AI and what should remain human.

02

Description

Communicate the task, context, constraints and desired outcome clearly.

03

Discernment

Evaluate, verify and challenge AI output instead of accepting confident-sounding answers.

04

Diligence

Test, secure, document and improve the thing you are responsible for shipping.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need to know how to code?

No traditional software-development background is required. You will learn practical building workflows using AI-assisted and visual tools, while also learning enough underlying concepts to inspect, test and troubleshoot what you build.

Is this mostly theory?

No. The curriculum is deliberately project-based. You learn a concept, use it, build with it and test the result.

What will I have at the end?

Your target outcomes include a working project, an n8n automation, a WhatsApp AI agent, a capstone, GitHub/portfolio material, a demo experience and a completion certificate.

What if I get stuck?

Bring the error, screenshot or exact message. Troubleshooting is part of the program, and the WhatsApp community is there for questions and support.
